常見問題

一定要登入或建帳號嗎？

不用。所有紀錄先存在手機裡，只有在你要和另一位照顧者共記時，才需要用 Sign in with Apple 登入。

怎麼和另一半一起記？

設定 › 照顧者與邀請 › 產生邀請碼，對方在 Maobao 的「我有邀請碼」輸入即可。邀請碼 24 小時內有效。

換手機怎麼把資料帶過去？

沒有帳號：設定 › 匯出與備份 › 匯出備份 .maobao，把檔案存到 iCloud Drive 或傳到新手機，再用「從備份還原」。有共記的寶寶登入後會自動同步。

語音記錄會上傳嗎？

不會。語音辨識在你的手機上完成；錄音只在你開啟共記時同步給同一位寶寶的照顧者。

提醒沒有跳出來？

到 iOS 設定 › 通知 › Maobao 確認已允許通知；用藥提醒要在記錄時填「醫囑間隔」；下次餵奶提醒要在設定 › 提醒開啟。

Widget 或鎖定畫面沒有更新？

Widget 每分鐘更新一次；記了一筆後會立刻刷新。若還是沒變，長按 Widget 移除後重新加入。

怎麼刪除帳號與所有資料？

設定 › 關於 › 刪除帳號與所有資料。會刪掉伺服器上你擁有的寶寶、全部紀錄、附件與帳號，無法復原。沒有帳號的話則是「刪除所有本機資料」。

app 會給醫療建議嗎？

不會。Maobao 只記錄與整理你輸入的數字；任何用藥間隔都依你輸入的醫囑，生長曲線只標示百分位、不做判定。有疑慮請諮詢醫師。

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your phone. Sign in with Apple is only needed when you want to share a baby with another caregiver.

How do I share with my partner?

Settings › Caregivers & invite › create an invite code; your partner enters it under "I have an invite code". Codes are valid for 24 hours.

How do I move to a new phone?

Without an account: Settings › Export & Backup › Export backup (.maobao), save it to iCloud Drive or send it to the new phone, then use Restore from backup. Shared babies sync automatically after signing in.

Are voice recordings uploaded?

No. Speech recognition runs on your device. Recordings sync only to the same baby's caregivers when sharing is on.

How do I delete my account and data?

Settings › About › Delete account and all data. This permanently removes the babies you own, all entries, attachments and the account. Without an account, use Delete all local data.

Does the app give medical advice?

No. Maobao only records and organizes what you enter. Medication intervals follow what you type from the prescription; growth curves show percentiles without judgement. Please consult your doctor with any concern.
